pdf‑show GitHub

Present PDFs like slides.

Drop a 16:9 PDF. It goes fullscreen and plays like a real slideshow: click to advance, smooth transitions, nothing to install.

Your file is never uploaded. Everything runs in your browser.

Click to advance

Left click goes forward, right click goes back. Just like PowerPoint.

Pixel-faithful

Rendered at your screen's resolution with the original aspect ratio. No cropping, no distortion.

Free forever

Open source, no ads, no tracking, no accounts. Works in every modern browser.
